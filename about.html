Who We Are

Serving people with compassion, truth and purpose.

Apostle Cornelius Hamilton is originally from Miami, Florida, and has lived in Tampa since adolescence. A graduate of The River Bible Institute, he has a passion for the lost and lives a life focused on soul winning.

Though in ministry for over a decade, he founded True Prosperity Evangelist Outreach in 2013: a Spirit-led ministry with a full-time feeding mission, clothing closet, food pantry, employment preparation, AA/NA/transitional housing referrals, spiritual guidance and weekly outreaches.

With the motto “God Over Everything,” he uses his background in the music industry and his influence in the streets to compel the lost to follow Christ, taking ministry from the seats of the church to the streets of his community.

Founder & Servant Leader
Meet the founder

Apostle Cornelius Hamilton

Born in Miami and raised in Tampa, Apostle Cornelius has spent over a decade taking the church from the pews to the streets. Since 2013 he has fed families, prayed with strangers and given hope to people others walked past.

His belief is simple: a hungry person needs a meal first, and then a friend who will pray with them. That is what God Over Everything means in action.

“I was hungry and you gave me something to eat.”Matthew 25:35

Mrs. Hamilton

A former employee of 95.7 The Beat (now iHeart Radio), Nia Broadcasting WTMP 1150 AM & 96.7 FM in Tampa and WRXB 1590 AM in St. Pete. A reformed gossip blogger, she has devoted her life to increasing the Kingdom of God.

Originally from Fayetteville, North Carolina, she moved to Tampa as a young teenager and partners with her husband in operating the ministry.

  • Spiritual guidance and life coaching
  • Community outreach and neighborhood care
