Faith Voucher

A voucher that puts food on the table with dignity.

How it works

1

Sponsor

Supporters donate to sponsor Faith Vouchers. Each $10 voucher is a gift for a family in need.

2

Receive

The ministry hands vouchers to people in need during outreaches and pantry hours.

3

Redeem

The family brings the voucher to the pantry or a participating store and receives groceries (ration) in return.

Please note: The Faith Voucher is a ministry gift voucher. It is not United States currency and not legal tender, and it has no cash value. It is redeemable only for food and grocery items through True Prosperity Evangelist Outreach and participating partners.
