Good to know

Frequently asked questions

When and where are the hot meals?

Hot meals and groceries are served every Monday, Wednesday and Saturday at 5 PM at 8751 N 40th St, Tampa, FL 33604.

What is Church in the Park?

An outdoor Sunday service at 2:45 PM. Call (813) 965-7796 for the exact location.

What is the Faith Voucher?

A ministry gift voucher that can be redeemed for groceries through the ministry and participating partners. It is not legal tender and has no cash value.

How can I volunteer or donate?

Use the form above, call (813) 965-7796 or email trueprosperityeoutreach@gmail.com and we will respond personally.

How do I help with the kidney appeal?

Please contact the ministry using the details on the Kidney Appeal page. Medical eligibility is decided by a transplant center.
